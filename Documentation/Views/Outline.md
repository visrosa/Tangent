Each independently scrolling note panel has an outline attached to its left edge, before the line gutter. The outline lists the note's [[Headers]] in order and indents subsections. Repeated heading names remain separate navigation targets.

Click a heading to move to that section. Headings inside folded sections remain listed and become visible when selected. The current section is highlighted as you move the text cursor or scroll through the note. Edits update the outline immediately.

The outline scrolls independently of the note. Use its top button to collapse it to a small rail or expand it again. Expansion is saved per note. Narrow panels start with the rail to leave room for editing; expanding it there opens an overlay that closes after choosing a heading. Widening the panel restores the saved preference.

The outline belongs to the panel rather than the document: it adds no Markdown, dirty state, or editing history. [[Feed Lens]] entries share their surrounding scrolling view and do not each receive a sidecar.
