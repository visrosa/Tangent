The Language & Input settings are found in the [[System Menu]].

# Editing Conventions
Enabling the __Smart Paragraph Breaks__ option will automatically insert a blank line when you press Enter in a paragraph. Additionally, deleting a paragraph with Backspace in front of any empty line will delete the empty line automatically.

When this setting is enabled, holding Shift while pressing Enter or Backspace will avoid this automatic behavior.

Enabling **Change List Glyphs on Indent** will automatically change the glyph used for a list when you indent a list under a parent. See [[Lists]] for more information.

# Quotation Marks
Enabling **Typographic Quotes** makes Tangent offer typographic quotation marks when you type a straight `"` or `'`. Nothing is replaced until you accept the suggestion, and existing text is never rewritten.

The **Typographic Quotes** list chooses which marks to offer. Each entry shows its marks for primary quotations and for quotations inside them, followed by the languages that use them. The entry for your system language is preselected, and saved when you first turn the setting on. **Custom** lets you pick each mark yourself; straight quotes and characters with Markdown meaning, such as `*` or `[`, are rejected. After a letter, a straight `'` offers an apostrophe first.

# Substitutions
Substitutions are listed by category, each pair showing what you type and what it offers: `->` and similar sequences offer an arrow, `--` or `---` inside a line offers an en or em dash, and `..` offers an ellipsis. Each category can be set to **Suggest** or **Off**.

# Spell Check
The __Use Spell Check__ toggle will control whether or not spell check should be enabled across your notes. __Spell Check Filenames__ and __Spell Check Content__ choose whether spell check is never shown, shown only while editing, or always shown.

__Additional Spell Check Languages__ lets you add additional languages to your spell check dictionary in addition to the default language of your system. Unfortunately, this option is _not_ available on Mac, where the languages follow your system settings.

## Custom Words
The words you have added to the custom dictionary are listed at the bottom of the page, titled with their count. See [[Dictionary]] for details.
