The Language & Spelling settings are found in the [[System Menu]].

# Spelling
The __Use Spell Check__ toggle will control whether or not spell check should be enabled across your notes. __Spell Check Filenames__ and __Spell Check Content__ choose whether spell check is never shown, shown only while editing, or always shown.

__Additional Spell Check Languages__ lets you add additional languages to your spell check dictionary in addition to the default language of your system. Unfortunately, this option is _not_ available on Mac.

# Editing Conventions
Enabling the __Smart Paragraph Breaks__ option will automatically insert a blank line when you press Enter in a paragraph. Additionally, deleting a paragraph with Backspace in front of any empty line will delete the empty line automatically.

When this setting is enabled, holding Shift while pressing Enter or Backspace will avoid this automatic behavior.

Enabling **Change List Glyphs on Indent** will automatically change the glyph used for a list when you indent a list under a parent. See [[Lists]] for more information.

# Quotation Marks
Enabling **Typographic Quotes** makes Tangent offer typographic quotation marks when you type a straight `"` or `'`. Nothing is replaced until you accept the suggestion, and existing text is never rewritten.

Choose the **Series** to offer: opening and closing marks for primary quotations, and for quotations inside them. The first series is preselected from your system language, and saved when you first turn the setting on. **Custom** lets you pick each mark yourself; straight quotes and characters with Markdown meaning, such as `*` or `[`, are rejected. After a letter, a straight `'` offers an apostrophe first.

# Substitutions
Typing `->`, `=>` and similar sequences offers an arrow, `--` or `---` inside a line offers an en or em dash, and `..` offers an ellipsis. Each group can be set to **Suggest** or **Off**.
