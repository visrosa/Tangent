The custom dictionary is listed in the [[Language & Input]] settings, found in the [[System Menu]].

Tangent uses Chromium's integration with your OS dictionary to provide spell checking. As part of that built-in functionality, you can add words Tangent thinks are misspelled to the custom dictionary.

Words are listed alphabetically in a scrollable grid that fills its rows from left to right. When the list is long enough to scroll, a column of letters appears beside it to jump to a letter. Use the field above the list to add a word, and the button beside a word to remove it; this is useful when a word was added by mistake.
